## Short description
Protect your documents, manage secure shares, and access your vault with biometric app lock.

## Full description
SecureID gives you a secure place to manage personal documents and control how you share them.

**Your document vault**
- Upload and view documents linked to your account.
- Download or delete your own stored documents.
- Access is protected by your account session and verification.

**Secure sharing**
- Create time-limited document shares.
- Revoke a share when you no longer want it to be accessible.
- Share only what you choose and keep links private.

**Security center**
- Review account sessions and security activity.
- Manage active sessions and revoke sessions you no longer recognize.
- Use email verification and multi-factor authentication where enabled.

**Biometric app lock**
- Use your supported device's biometric authentication to unlock the app.
- Biometric authentication is an additional local app-access control; it does not replace your SecureID account password or server-side authentication.

SecureID is a document-management and sharing tool. It does not currently certify the authenticity of PAN, Aadhaar, passports, academic certificates, or other government-issued documents. Do not rely on it as a government identity verification service.
