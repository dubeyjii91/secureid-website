Delete your SecureID account

You can request permanent deletion of your SecureID account from the Android app or from the SecureID website. You do not need to keep the Android app installed to use the website deletion process.

Delete from the Android app

  1. Sign in to SecureID and complete email MFA.
  2. Open Security Center.
  3. Scroll to Account deletion.
  4. Enter your current password and choose Delete account permanently.
  5. Review the confirmation and confirm deletion.

Request deletion from the website

  1. Open SecureID in a web browser and sign in.
  2. Complete email MFA if requested.
  3. Open Account security from the account navigation.
  4. In Export or delete your account, enter your current password and choose Permanently delete account.

What deletion removes

After your password and MFA checks succeed, SecureID deletes your account, identity profile, uploaded document records and encrypted document files, active shares, sessions, recovery codes, and associated security history from active systems.

Encrypted backup copies may remain until they expire under the configured backup-retention schedule. The deletion cannot be undone. Download any data you want to keep before confirming.

For account safety, SecureID requires you to sign in and confirm deletion with your current password. If you cannot sign in, use the password-recovery option on the sign-in page before submitting the deletion request.
