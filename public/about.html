Getting started with SecureID.

Who can use SecureID?

Individuals can use the available personal account features. Institution workspaces and their tools depend on institution access and account permissions.

Can I share documents?

SecureID includes Secure Sharing tools. Check the recipient and access details shown in your account before sharing.

Where do I manage my account?

Open the SecureID application to sign in, manage your documents and access the security features available to your account.
